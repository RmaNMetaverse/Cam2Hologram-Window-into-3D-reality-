HeadTracking Hologram3D

Turn any webcam into a window into 3D space.

MediaPipe Face Mesh tracks your eyes and head in real space. The scene is then rendered with an off‑axis (asymmetric) projection locked to your eye position, so the model counter‑rotates against your movement — move left and its right side swings toward you, exactly like a real object sitting in front of your monitor.

  • 1 Allow camera access when prompted.
  • 2 Set your screen size in Calibration for a correct illusion.
  • 3 Close one eye — the effect is strongest monocular.
